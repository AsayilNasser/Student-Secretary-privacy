Thank you for choosing to use the Student Secretary app. This Privacy Policy is intended to inform you about how we handle your data. 

## Data Collection and Usage
The Student Secretary app is designed to help students manage their time, track study sessions using a timer, and view their achievements. 

We respect your privacy. The Student Secretary app does not collect, store, or share any personal information or user data. All data entered into the app (such as study times, tasks, and achievements) is stored locally on your device. We do not use any external servers to process or store your personal information.

## Analytics and Third-Party Services
